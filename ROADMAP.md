# ADN Network roadmap — course set on 10/07/2026

## Live foundation (verifiable in the application)
- Application installed on the client machine; local computation;
  data kept on the machine.
- Every control produces a timestamped digital fingerprint,
  viewable in the application.
- Sending handled by the sending service operated by Packdata,
  tracking returned in the application.

## Course
- Public anchoring: daily publication of a fingerprint via public
  timestamping — third-party verifiable proof.
- Free contributor app: verification by contributor machines —
  decentralized architecture.
- Deadline and mechanism published at each step.

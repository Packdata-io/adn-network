**ABSTRACT**

## The problem: data that ages the moment you buy it

Most B2B databases are **frozen files**: accurate on the day of purchase, outdated a few months later. A database loses on average nearly **30% freshness per year** — bouncing emails, contacts who changed roles, wasted time.

ADN Network takes a different approach: **living data** that refreshes while you use it, and every update is **tracked, sealed, and rewarded**.

**THE NETWORK**

## A living network, not a one-time file delivery

Everyone's freshness benefits all: the more the network is used, the more up-to-date the data is.

Data refreshes as the network is used. You never prospect on an outdated database.

Every data point is verified. You reach real people, up to date, across 110 countries.

Your queries and data never leave your device. Only **hashes** travel the network — never raw data.

**TWO ROLES, ONE NETWORK**

## Businesses consume, contributors refresh

A virtuous circle: those who use the data and those who keep it up to date, connected by the same network, the ADN Network.

BUSINESS — Licensed: uses **Packdata Lite** (free) + **Packdata Desktop** (ADN Network license) to prospect and contact decision makers on fresh data. Access to all B2B data + AI + sending. Unlimited extractions per plan. Using the app also refreshes the network.

Company employees: use **Packdata Lite** (free, PC/Mac/iOS/Android/Chrome), the app that feeds the network with updates and earns extraction rights. Increase the company's extractions and reduce the price of its annual renewal.

CONTRIBUTOR — Individual: uses **Packdata Lite** (free): the app randomly queries public data to confirm or refresh it in ADN Network. Each contribution is signed and timestamped. Earns extraction rights with each contribution, accumulates extraction credits (year 2).

**The participative principle:** every **Packdata Lite** validates and refreshes public records in the background — the more users, the more accurate and self-cleaning the database becomes in real time — in the manner of Waze (a Google trademark, mentioned solely to explain, with no affiliation).

**CERTIFICATION**

## Every data point can be sealed and timestamped

Information sourced from a **public source** can be certified: a **SHA-256** hash (integrity), an **ECDSA P-256** signature verifiable by third parties (shareable public key), and **chaining** — each block points to the previous one, with no possible erasure.

We are deliberately measured: we speak of **sealed · verifiable integrity**, not "tamper-proof". The chain is currently internal; a **daily hash will be publicly anchored** (see roadmap) to strengthen its traceability.

**ADN Network certifies each data point with a certificate**, integrated into the application.

**At group scale:** subsidiaries, holdings and Sherlock-derived org charts are sealed the same way, at every level.

**Key point:** only **hashes** travel the chain, never raw data.

*Figure — Example output of the Packdata software, sealed in the ADN Network — identity masked, authentic figures and sources.*

A certificate contains: verified identity, public data (subscribers, reliability /20, generic email, HQ), at least 3 opposing sources (public profile, engine indexing, open-data SIREN), fingerprint — unique ID, SHA-256, third-party-verifiable ECDSA P-256 signature, shareable public key, HMAC signature, chaining (block + previous block), UTC timestamp. Each request freezes a dated state; the certificate chain makes the history, up or down.

Frugal light client by design: requests throttled at 40 KB max (fragmented data), user-adjustable pace (cycle interval, delay between requests, AI-driven auto mode) with upfront impact estimate — zero impact on data plans or battery at standard settings. Each check is declared, visible and can be disabled at any time.

**PROTOCOL GOVERNANCE**

## What runs alone, what requires a signature

**Cerveau Numérique** is the decision and update protocol of the **ADN Network**: compiled local-first software, P2P network with native streaming, no central cloud, no single point of failure.

Rule 1: **what can run unattended runs unattended**. Rule 2: **what creates liability requires a signature**. Every release is logged, verifiable, reversible.

Infrastructure costs offloaded to the client machine, fixed lifetime licence model. Enforceable detail in data room under NDA.

## Contribute to freshness, earn rights

Every time a contributor **confirms or refreshes** a public professional data point, their contribution is **signed**, timestamped and chained. The network thus verifiably knows who contributed to what — without ever exposing raw data.

These contributions open extraction rights: the more you contribute to freshness, the more you can extract.

1. Contribution: contributor confirms or refreshes data → earns extraction rights. More contributions = fresher data for all.
2. Extraction: business uses its rights to extract data, per its plan. Usage also refreshes the network.
3. Cycle: the more active the network → fresh data → everyone benefits. A virtuous circle: shared freshness.
4. Balance: businesses extract per their plan, the network stays fresh. Everyone prospects on up-to-date data.
